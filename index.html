    my dearest Tanu,

    There are things I want to say that I have been carrying quietly for a long time, things I feel most clearly when I look at you and watch that blush creep in before you can hide it. That blush is my favourite thing in the world. It means you know exactly what you mean to me, and you still cannot quite believe it. Baby, believe it.

    I think about the main road, how something as ordinary as a road became extraordinary the moment we were on it together. I think about Neem Café, the soft clatter of it, and how I did not want that night to ever end. I think about Shamiyana, and everything that passed between us there, every quiet second of it is folded up somewhere safe inside me, and I go back to it often.

    I love your eyes, the way they move when you are thinking, the warmth in them when you laugh, the softness when they find mine. I love your maturity, the quiet depth of you, the way you carry yourself through the world with such grace. I love the way you pamper me without making it a thing, it just comes from you naturally, like breathing, like it is just who you are.

    I love your cute ears, your nose, that smile, and God, those dimples. I could write a hundred letters about your dimples alone. And I love the way you love me, Tanu. So fully, so gently, so completely, as if it costs you nothing, as if it is the most natural thing you have ever done. It is the greatest gift anyone has ever given me.

    You are not just someone I love. You are the standard. You are the reason I stopped looking. On every ordinary day, walking the same road, sitting in the same light, you make everything feel like the very beginning of something beautiful.

    I do not have grand words for what you are to me. I just know this: I would choose this, every single time. I would choose you.

    yours, completely and always
    Adii
